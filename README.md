A make.fun entry for **The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse**.

`main.py` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — the excuse a dog gives
after being caught eating the homework, in the dog's own voice:

> You left the homework on the floor. The floor is my plate. I was only doing the dishes.

The dog believes it has done nothing wrong: the homework was on the floor,
floor food is dog food, and the dishes needed doing.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.py` validates the line before writing it: 1–199 characters, a single
line, no leading/trailing whitespace, no double spaces, about the homework,
in the first person, with a setup and a punch, and none of the stock lines
the judge's brief counts against (the cat, the taste of paper, being a good
boy).
